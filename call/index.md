*This is a preliminary call. The full call, with submission categories, page limits, the review process, important dates, and the submission website, will be published soon.*

SAIV 2027 invites submissions on all aspects of AI verification, broadly construed, presenting theoretical results, novel algorithms, tools, benchmarks, case studies, and empirical evaluations. We welcome contributions from academia and industry, and from the formal methods, AI, machine learning, security, and control communities.

## Important Dates

To be announced. The SAIV week is planned for October 31 – November 5, 2027; see [SAIV Week]({{ site.baseurl }}/week/).

## Topics

The topics covered by SAIV include, but are not limited to, the following:

##### Formal methods for artificial intelligence

- Safety specifications for systems with learning components
- Formal verification of neural networks
- Symbolic analysis of cyber-physical systems with AI components
- Neuro-symbolic reasoning for AI safety
- Testing approaches for systems with AI components
- Formal guarantees for interpretable AI

##### Safety and security of frontier and agentic AI

- Verification, monitoring, and runtime assurance of AI agents and multi-agent systems
- Cybersecurity risks of agentic AI, such as prompt injection, tool misuse, and data exfiltration
- Adversarial attacks, robustness, and certified defenses
- Specifications, guardrails, and guarantees for large language models and foundation models
- Safety and security of AI-generated code
- Quantitative and probabilistic safety guarantees for AI systems

##### Artificial intelligence for formal methods

- Machine learning for program synthesis and control synthesis
- Machine learning and large language models for automated reasoning and theorem proving
- Differentiable proof certificates
- Statistical approaches to falsification and verification
- Data-driven verification

##### AI verification in practice

- Industrial case studies and lessons learned in verifying and deploying AI systems
- Tools, benchmarks, and competitions for AI verification
