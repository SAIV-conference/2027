## Mission

AI is developing at an unprecedented pace and scale, and its weaknesses are exposed when we try to build trustworthy systems, particularly in safety- and security-critical domains. Verification, and formal methods more broadly, develop methodologies and techniques that provide guarantees about systems. Combining AI and verification is therefore natural and timely, and it carries enormous potential for bringing AI systems into practice safely.

As AI systems become agents that plan, write and execute code, and interact with tools and with each other, the stakes grow. Agentic AI raises new safety and cybersecurity risks, from prompt injection and tool misuse to insecure AI-generated code, alongside long-standing challenges such as adversarial attacks on machine-learning models. The Symposium on AI Verification (SAIV) is dedicated to the science and engineering of guarantees for such systems.

Research on AI and verification is currently scattered across many conferences in formal methods, AI, machine learning, programming languages, security, and control, and much of the cutting-edge work happens in industry. SAIV aims to bring these communities together. Its long-term goals are to be:

1. **A leading venue for publishing** high-quality research on AI and verification, broadly construed.
2. **A meeting point for the community**, where academic and industrial researchers exchange ideas, with a special emphasis on students and early-career researchers.

## History

SAIV grew out of two workshop series, the Workshop on Formal Methods for ML-Enabled Autonomous Systems ([FoMLAS](https://fomlas2023.wixsite.com/fomlas2023), 2018–2023) and the Workshop on Open Problems in Learning and Verification of Neural Networks ([WOLVERINE](https://wolverine-workshop.github.io/Wolverine2023/), 2021–2023), which merged into SAIV in 2024. SAIV 2027 is the **10th edition** of the series. SAIV 2024, 2025, and 2026 were co-located with the International Conference on Computer Aided Verification (CAV; in 2026, at the Federated Logic Conference, FLoC) and published their proceedings in Springer's *Lecture Notes in Computer Science* (LNCS); SAIV 2027 is the first standalone edition. SAIV also hosts the International Verification of Neural Networks Competition ([VNN-COMP]({{ site.baseurl }}/vnn-comp/)).

All past editions, with links to their websites and proceedings, are listed on the [History]({{ site.baseurl }}/history/) page.

## Organization

The [steering committee]({{ site.baseurl }}/committees/#steering-committee) provides long-term continuity, strategic guidance, and governance, including selecting the program chairs. The [program chairs]({{ site.baseurl }}/committees/#program-chairs) are responsible for each year's edition: they select the program committee, manage paper reviewing and selection, and organize the event.
