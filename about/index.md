## Mission

AI is developing at an unprecedented pace and scale, and its weaknesses are exposed when we try to build trustworthy systems, particularly in safety- and security-critical domains. Verification, and formal methods more broadly, develop methodologies and techniques that provide guarantees about systems. Combining AI and verification is therefore natural and timely, and it carries enormous potential for bringing AI systems into practice safely.

As AI systems become agents that plan, write and execute code, and interact with tools and with each other, the stakes grow. Agentic AI raises new safety and cybersecurity risks, from prompt injection and tool misuse to insecure AI-generated code, alongside long-standing challenges such as adversarial attacks on machine-learning models. SAIV is dedicated to the science and engineering of guarantees for such systems.

Research on AI and verification is currently scattered across many conferences in formal methods, AI, machine learning, programming languages, security, and control, and much of the cutting-edge work happens in industry. SAIV aims to bring these communities together. Its long-term goals are to be:

1. **A leading venue for publishing** high-quality research on AI and verification, broadly construed.
2. **A meeting point for the community**, where academic and industrial researchers exchange ideas, with a special emphasis on students and early-career researchers.

## History

SAIV grew out of two workshop series, [FoMLAS](https://fomlas2023.wixsite.com/fomlas2023) (2018–2023) and [WOLVERINE](https://wolverine-workshop.github.io/Wolverine2023/) (2021–2023), and has been held as the Symposium on AI Verification since 2024.

- **SAIV 2024**, Montreal, Canada, co-located with CAV 2024
- **SAIV 2025**, Zagreb, Croatia, co-located with CAV 2025
- **SAIV 2026**, Lisbon, Portugal, co-located with FLoC 2026
- **SAIV 2027**, London, United Kingdom, the first standalone edition

The proceedings of SAIV 2024–2026 appeared in Springer's LNCS series. Earlier editions are linked from the *Editions* menu.

## Organization

The [steering committee]({{ site.baseurl }}/committees/#steering-committee) provides long-term continuity, strategic guidance, and governance, including selecting the program chairs. The [program chairs]({{ site.baseurl }}/committees/#program-chairs) are responsible for each year's edition: they select the program committee, manage paper reviewing and selection, and organize the event.
