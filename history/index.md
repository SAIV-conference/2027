The Symposium on AI Verification (SAIV) grew out of two workshop series, the Workshop on Formal Methods for ML-Enabled Autonomous Systems (FoMLAS, 2018–2023) and the Workshop on Open Problems in Learning and Verification of Neural Networks (WOLVERINE, 2021–2023), which merged into SAIV in 2024. Edition numbers continue the FoMLAS count, so SAIV 2024 was the 7th edition and SAIV 2027 is the **10th**. SAIV 2024, 2025, and 2026 were co-located with the International Conference on Computer Aided Verification (CAV), and their proceedings appeared in Springer's *Lecture Notes in Computer Science* (LNCS).
