AI systems are advancing at an unprecedented pace and are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents that write code, use tools, and act in the world. The Symposium on AI Verification (SAIV) brings together researchers and practitioners from formal methods, artificial intelligence, machine learning, security, and control to develop rigorous techniques that make AI systems safe, secure, and trustworthy. Its topics range from the verification of neural networks and robustness against adversarial attacks to guarantees and safeguards for large language models and agentic AI.

## The 10th edition of SAIV

SAIV 2027 is the **tenth edition** of a series that began in 2018. The Workshop on Formal Methods for ML-Enabled Autonomous Systems ([FoMLAS](https://fomlas2023.wixsite.com/fomlas2023), 2018–2023) and the Workshop on Open Problems in Learning and Verification of Neural Networks ([WOLVERINE](https://wolverine-workshop.github.io/Wolverine2023/), 2021–2023) merged into SAIV in 2024, and SAIV has since been held three times, each co-located with the International Conference on Computer Aided Verification (CAV) and with proceedings in Springer's *Lecture Notes in Computer Science*:

- **SAIV 2024**, Montreal, Canada, with [CAV 2024](https://web.archive.org/web/20251220222927/https://i-cav.org/2024/) · [proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-65112-0)
- **SAIV 2025**, Zagreb, Croatia, with [CAV 2025](https://conferences.i-cav.org/2025/) · [proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-99991-8)
- **SAIV 2026**, Lisbon, Portugal, with [CAV 2026](https://conferences.i-cav.org/2026/) at the Federated Logic Conference ([FLoC 2026](https://www.floc26.org/)) · [proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-32357-6)

After three editions at CAV, SAIV 2027 will be held as a **standalone event** for the first time: a week of events in **London, United Kingdom**, built around the main conference. SAIV 2027 is also planned to host the 8th International Verification of Neural Networks Competition ([VNN-COMP 2027]({{ site.baseurl }}/vnn-comp/)). See [History]({{ site.baseurl }}/history/) for all past editions.
