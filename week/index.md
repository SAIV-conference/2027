SAIV 2027 is organized as a week of events in London, United Kingdom. *All dates are preliminary and subject to change; details will be announced on this website.*

<div class="week-event" markdown="1">
<div class="week-date">Sunday, October 31 – Monday, November 1</div>

### Autumn School
{: #autumn-school}

A two-day school for PhD students, early-career researchers, and practitioners, covering the foundations and the state of the art of AI verification. We also plan mentoring activities for students and early-career researchers.
</div>

<div class="week-event" markdown="1">
<div class="week-date">Tuesday, November 2 – Wednesday, November 3</div>

### Main Conference
{: #main-conference}

Keynotes, invited talks, and presentations of peer-reviewed papers. See the [call for papers]({{ site.baseurl }}/call/).
</div>

<div class="week-event" markdown="1">
<div class="week-date">Thursday, November 4</div>

### Research Highlights
{: #research-highlights}

A day dedicated to highlights of recent research on AI verification.
</div>

## Supporting SAIV

SAIV 2027 welcomes support from organizations that share its mission. Sponsorship helps us support student participation, the autumn school, and travel grants. Organizations interested in sponsoring are invited to contact the [program chairs]({{ site.baseurl }}/committees/#program-chairs) or the [steering committee]({{ site.baseurl }}/committees/#steering-committee).
