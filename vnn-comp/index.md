The **International Verification of Neural Networks Competition (VNN-COMP)** is held annually to enable a fair and objective comparison of state-of-the-art neural network verification tools, to encourage the standardization of tool interfaces, and to bring together the neural network verification community. Participants run their tools on a shared set of benchmarks proposed by the community, and the results are presented at the event that hosts the competition. VNN-COMP has been part of the Symposium on AI Verification (SAIV) since 2024, and from 2021 to 2023 it was part of SAIV's predecessor, the Workshop on Formal Methods for ML-Enabled Autonomous Systems (FoMLAS); see [History]({{ site.baseurl }}/history/).

## VNN-COMP 2027
{: #vnn-comp-2027}

The **8th International Verification of Neural Networks Competition (VNN-COMP 2027)** is planned as part of SAIV 2027 in London. It has not been announced yet; the call for benchmarks and participation, the rules, and the timeline will be published on the [VNN-COMP website](https://vnn-comp.github.io/).
